About me

I'm Md. Mahinur Rahman Sun, a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering student at Southeast University in Dhaka. My focus is frontend development: turning ideas into responsive, well-structured interfaces that work on every screen.

I learn by building. Each concept I study becomes a small project that I deploy live and publish on GitHub. I work with AI tools to move faster, and I make a point of understanding every line before it ships.

I care about clean code, clear communication and careful finishing, and I'm looking to keep growing alongside people who care about the same things.
